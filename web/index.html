How it works

From legal text to a clear risk report

Five steps run behind the Analyze button, using the same Python engine as the desktop app.

1

Add your document

Upload a PDF, DOCX or TXT file, paste text, or snap a photo.

2

Read and clean

The text is extracted and split into individual clauses.

3

Classify

TF-IDF features and Logistic Regression label every clause.

4

Score

Each risk gets a severity, and the document gets a score out of 100.

5

Explain

Plain-language reasons and recommendations, plus an optional PDF.

The model

Trained on 336 labelled clauses across 10 risk categories plus a No Risk class. Cross-validated accuracy is 81.55% (±3.14%), measured with 5-fold cross-validation repeated 3 times.

Good to know

  • Results show possible risks only. This is not legal advice.
  • Accuracy depends on the training data, and some categories are weaker than others.
  • Photo reading depends on picture quality.
  • One clause may occasionally be counted twice when sentences are split.
